    ICTIS 2025, International Conference on Information and Communication Technology for Intelligent Systems

    In May 2025 I presented my paper A YOLO Model for Car License Plates Detection and Recognition, written with Dr. Eva Tuba and Dr. Milan Tuba, at ICTIS 2025 at Cornell University's NYC-ILR Conference Center in New York. Most license plate research tests plates from only one country. The main goal of the research is to see how well YOLO object detection models handle very different plate styles and image qualities, using datasets from China (CCPD), Brazil (UFPR-ALPR), and Taiwan (AOLP), plus a combined set of all three. The trained models were great at detecting license plates, scoring above 98 out of 100 (mAP50 above 0.98), but drawing a box that fits the plate's exact edges was harder (mAP50-95 of 0.60 to 0.73), which is where I see the most room for future work.

    Paper: Springer, Smart Innovation, Systems and Technologies, vol. 126 · Code: GitHub
